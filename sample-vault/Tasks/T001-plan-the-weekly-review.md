# Plan the weekly review

Fix one weekly slot: what moved, what is stuck, which three cards come next.

Why: a board only helps if someone looks at it on a schedule.

Done when:
- The slot is in the calendar
- The checklist below is used for two weeks in a row

Checklist:
- Review the board: what moved, what is stuck
- Pick three cards for the coming week
- Note anything that needs a decision
