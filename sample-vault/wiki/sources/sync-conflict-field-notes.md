# Field notes, a month of shared notes

## Summary
Short notes by [[ben-okafor]] about a team of three sharing one folder of Markdown files for a month: how often sync
conflicts happened, how long they took to settle and what reduced them. Invented demo text.

## Key claims
- Conflicts happened about once a week, usually on a page that two people edited ([[sync-conflicts]]).
- Most were settled in under five minutes; one lost an hour of edits.
- Splitting big pages into many small files made conflicts less likely.
- Plain text made each conflict readable, with both versions side by side ([[plain-text-files]]).

⚠️ Contradiction: [[local-first-software-notes]] says sync conflicts are rare, while this source reports about one a week.
The scopes differ (three writers against one). Not decided; see [[sync-conflicts]].
