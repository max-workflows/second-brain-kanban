# Field notes: a month of shared notes

Author: Ben Okafor (fictional). Date: 2026-11-12. This is invented demo text.

Our team of three shared one folder of Markdown notes for a month.

- Sync conflicts happened about once a week, usually when two people edited the same index page.
- Most conflicts were settled in under five minutes; one lost an hour of edits.
- Splitting big pages into many small files made conflicts much less likely.
- Plain text made every conflict readable: we could see both versions side by side.

Our conclusion: plain files are a good base for a shared knowledge base, but a shared folder needs rules about who edits
which page, and small files help.
